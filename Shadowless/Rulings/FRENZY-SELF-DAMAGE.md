# "Even to itself" reaches the Confusion penalty

One ruling out of [RULINGS.md](../RULINGS.md) — the directory, the four-step order for making a new
one, and the principles index all live there. **Append-only: correct this entry if it turns out
wrong, never condense it.** See [MAINTENANCE.md](../MAINTENANCE.md).

---

**Settled with Trevor 17 Aug 2026.** Dark Primeape's Frenzy:

> If Dark Primeape does any damage while it's Confused (even to itself), it does 30 more damage.

**A Confused Dark Primeape that fails its attack takes 60**, not 30. Confusion's failed-attack
self-hit is 30 in this engine, and Frenzy adds its 30 on top. Against 70 HP, one bad coin leaves it
on 10.

## Why

**The parenthetical has to be describing something, and this is the only thing it can describe.**
Dark Primeape prints no recoil and no self-damaging attack. *"Even to itself"* refers to nothing at
all on this card unless it refers to the Confusion penalty — so the narrow reading, where Frenzy only
boosts damage dealt to opponents, has WotC writing a clause about an empty set.

**The card is built as a liability and the number is the point.** Frenzied Attack confuses Dark
Primeape *on purpose*, every single time it is used — the card puts itself into the state Frenzy keys
off. Filing the 60 down to 30 takes the risk out of a card whose entire design is that risk.

## What it commits us to

**A "does N more damage" modifier reaches damage from any source unless the card restricts it**,
including damage the rules inflict rather than damage you chose to deal. That is broader than it
sounds, so the boundary matters: the modifier attaches to damage this Pokémon **does**, which is why
it reaches the Confusion hit — the rules have Dark Primeape hit *itself*, and it is the one doing it.
It does not reach damage merely **taken**, so a Poison tick between turns or an opponent's attack is
untouched.

**Watch for the shape rather than for this card.** Neo's Baby Pokémon and several later passives
carry blanket damage modifiers with the same "any damage" phrasing, and which non-attack sources they
reach will be asked again. The answer here is: the ones where this Pokémon is the one doing the
damage.

**One consequence to expect in the log rather than treat as a bug.** A Confused Dark Primeape taking
60 from a failed attack, then being Knocked Out by it, hands the opponent a Prize for an attack that
never resolved. That is correct and it will look wrong the first time somebody reads it back.
