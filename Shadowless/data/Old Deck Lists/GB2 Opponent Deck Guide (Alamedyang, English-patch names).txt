=========================================================================
  [A1]  WHERE THE DATA CAME FROM
=========================================================================

The contents of all decks were determined by looking at the saved game
data using a hex editor, during or immediately after a duel with the
opponent in question.


=========================================================================
  [A2]  ABOUT THE NAMES
=========================================================================

Two names are given for many characters in this guide. The first name is
in English, the second name is in Japanese. English names are taken from
the first Pokemon TCG gameboy game when possible, the unofficial English
patch when not possible.
